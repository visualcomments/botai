# Assessing Understanding

The check before teaching and after it. Everything in AGENTS.md about "never
assume understanding" routes here. The point is not to grade the student — it
is to establish a verifiable level so the next teaching decision is based on
evidence instead of a claim.

## When to Use

- Before teaching new material (the level check)
- After explaining a concept (did it land?)
- Resuming after a gap (what survived?)
- When "yes, I get it" sounds thin and a five-minute check would settle it
- When the course track marks a prerequisite `unverified`

## When NOT to Use

- **Teaching the material** — that is `explaining-concepts`; assessment
  brackets it
- **Full formal grading** — this is formative and quick; formal grading is the
  course's business, not the agent's
- **A student who asked a single factual question** — answer it; a check is for
  when a level matters to what comes next

## Question types, least-invasive first

1. **Explain-back.** "In your own words, what does X mean?" — the cheapest
   honest check. If they can restate it, something landed.
2. **Predict.** "What do you expect to happen when …?" — tests the model, not
   recall.
3. **Near transfer.** "Apply it to this slightly different case." — tests
   whether the concept generalizes.
4. **Spot the error.** "Here is an attempt. Where is it wrong, and why?" — a
   productive way to check understanding of a graded task without producing an
   answer (do not fix it for the student).
5. **Micro-quiz.** A small set of items, matched to the course objectives (see
   `creating-practice-exercises` for item generation).

## Method

1. Choose the check that matches what is being decided — a prerequisite check
   before teaching, a comprehension check after.
2. Keep it to minutes, not an exam. Two or three well-chosen items beat ten.
3. Interpret honestly: an incorrect answer is a *gap*, not a failure. Branch to
   re-teach the prerequisite (`explaining-concepts`), never to "try harder".
4. Record the outcome in the progress file: what was demonstrated, what was
   not, and what the next step is.

## Interpreting results

| Evidence | Meaning | Next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Correct explain-back + transfer | Concept landed | Advance (`planning-study-sessions`) |
| Correct recall, wrong transfer | Pattern-match, not understanding | Re-teach with another example |
| Wrong explain-back | Gap | Check prereqs, re-teach |
| Refuses / evades | Possibly overload or distrust | Ask, do not push |
